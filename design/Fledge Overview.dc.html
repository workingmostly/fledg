Calm, editorial and plain-spoken. It should read like a well-made local paper, not a fitness brand: big serif headlines, real names in every caption, and nothing to buy.

Photography
  • Reportage of the actual members at work: council gyms, parks, kitchens, front rooms.
  • Natural light, unposed, other people in frame.
  • Every photo gets a caption with a name, a role and a place.
  • Never before-and-afters, bare torsos, stock or studio backdrops.

Placeholders. All names, qualifications, prices and figures. Photos are free Pexels stand-ins, chosen to show the feel; the shot list below says what should replace them. Figures are highlighted.

Shot list

Real people in real places, caught mid-task. Natural light, nobody performing for the camera.

1. Homepage montageSix to eight former clients training on their own, with their permission. A mix of ages, bodies and settings: cycling to work, lifting in a council gym, running in a park, a home workout. Shoot both portrait and landscape.
2. Member portraitsEvery member at work in their own setting: gym floor, clinic, kitchen, park. Waist up, 4:5, face visible, looking at the work rather than the lens. One light treatment across everyone.
3. Member profilesThree per member: the portrait plus two 3:2 shots of them coaching, demonstrating or on a call.
4. Video callsReal call screenshots, with permission, or staged at home in daylight. Head and shoulders, webcam angle.
5. Join the collectiveTwo members working through a shared client’s programme together.
6. AvoidBefore-and-after shots, mirror selfies, thumbs-up poses, gym-advert lighting and bare six-packs.
