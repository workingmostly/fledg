The collective

Choose one coach. Meet the people behind them.

Your coach is the person you talk to every week. When your goals need it, they’ll bring in a specialist from this page.

Everyone is qualified. We check certificates and insurance before anyone joins.

You pay your coach directly. Each member sets their own prices and Fledg takes none of it.

No lock-in. You can change coach, or stop, whenever you like.
