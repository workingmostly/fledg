Where the money goes

Fledg takes nothing. Here’s what it costs to run.

Clients pay their coach directly. Members pay a one-time fee when they join, and that fee covers running costs and nothing else. Every cost is listed below, and we update this page when they change.

Last updated 1 October 2026Highlighted figures are placeholders

Ways other people make money from coaching. We don’t.

No commission.

Members keep 100% of their fees. No booking fee or platform fee either.

No paid placement.

Members are listed in random order. Nobody can pay to appear higher.

No advertising.

No ads, no sponsored posts and no affiliate links to supplements or kit.

No subscriptions.

Members pay once. Clients never pay Fledg anything at all.

No data.

Your programme and logs stay in your own apps. We never hold them.

Questions about money

PDFAccounts, 2025 to 2026Placeholder file · published once filed
Is Fledg a charity?
No. Fledg is a not-for-profit collective, but it isn’t a registered charity. Clients never pay Fledg, and the joining fee only covers running costs.
How do I pay my coach?
Directly, by bank transfer or however you agree between you. Every member’s prices are on their profile, so you know the cost before you start.
What happens to money left over?
It’s carried into next year and lowers the joining fee for new members. We don’t save beyond the reserve.
What if costs go up?
We’ll explain why on this page before the fee changes. Existing members never pay again.
