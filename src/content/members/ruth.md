I spent eight years as a gym instructor watching people pay for sessions they didn’t understand. So I coach the other way round: explain everything, then hand it over.

Most of my clients work full-time, often at a desk. We train around that, not against it. Three sessions a week is usually plenty.

Outside work I lift, I’m slowly learning to swim properly, and I’ll talk about deadlifts for longer than anyone wants.
